Line of balance

Time runs left to right, location up the page in flow order. Each trade is one line; its slope is the production rate, so a steep line is a slow trade. Lines that converge are crews running out of room between them. The marker on a line is the binding location — the one place the buffer is fully consumed, and therefore where a slip is felt first.

It is an ordinary network
