Plan reliability

PPC is commitments met over commitments made, per week. It measures the reliability of the promise, not the volume of work — a crew that did twice what it promised is at 100%, because promising badly is the thing being measured. A week with anything unassessed reads unmeasurable rather than being scored on the part that was answered.

A commitment with a live constraint is refused, not warned about — that refusal is the method. Naming the blocker here does not store it: the whole commitment is rejected, and the message tells you what stopped it. Clear the blocker in the world, then promise the work.
